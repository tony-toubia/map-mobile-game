HOW TO PLAY

🎯 HUNTERS

Track down and eliminate the Monster before it fully evolves. Work with AI teammates and coordinate your attacks. Dead hunters respawn via Dropship after 30 seconds.

  • Assault: High damage dealer - grenades and rockets
  • Trapper: Controls the battlefield - traps and mobile arena
  • Medic: Keeps the team alive - heal beam and burst
  • Support: Shields, cloaking, and orbital strikes
  • Jetpack Dodge: Press Shift to boost-dodge away from danger

👹 MONSTERS

Feed on wildlife to gain evolution progress and armor. When ready, find a safe spot to evolve (5-second cocoon phase where you're vulnerable). At Stage 3, destroy the Power Relay to win!

  • Stage 1: Weak - focus on feeding and sneaking
  • Stage 2: Balanced - fight or feed strategically
  • Stage 3: Ultimate form - hunt or attack the Power Relay
  • Armor: Feeding grants armor that absorbs damage before health
  • Sneak (Shift): Move slowly while invisible to hunters
  • Smell (Q): Highlight nearby entities through obstacles
  • Evolve (E): When evolution bar is full, find safety and evolve

🎮 CONTROLS

Mobile: Left joystick moves, right joystick aims and auto-fires. Tap ability buttons for skills.

Desktop: WASD/Arrows to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot. Number keys 1-6 for abilities. Shift to dodge/sneak. Q for smell. E to evolve.

⚡ WIN CONDITIONS

  • Hunters win: Kill the Monster
  • Monster wins: Kill all hunters OR destroy the Power Relay (Stage 3)
  • Timer: If time runs out, the Monster wins
